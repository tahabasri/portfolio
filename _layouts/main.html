ABOUT ME

from Morocco, with code

I'm Taha, a developer, consultant and instructor who grew up in Meknes, one of Morocco's old imperial cities. I studied there and started my career there, and something about a place built by hand over centuries stuck with me: the patience, the craft, the love of getting the small details exactly right. I bring the same to the software I make.

My story with tech started young. I was a kid in 2004 the first time I sat in front of a computer, and I never really got up. That curiosity carried me from Java engineering in Morocco to the Salesforce platform, and eventually to Dubai, with a strong coffee (or a mint tea) never far away.

These days I build and teach on Salesforce: shipping the solutions behind great customer experiences, and sharing what I learn through talks, writing and open source. If you're trying to make something complex feel simple, that's exactly the kind of problem I love, so let's build something.
