## Bio

I'm Taha Basri, a Salesforce Technical Architect (Senior Technical Consultant at Salesforce),
developer, consultant and instructor. I grew up in **Meknes**, one of Morocco's old imperial
cities, studied and started my career there, and carry its craft mindset — patience and getting
the small details exactly right — into the software I build.

My story with tech started in **2004**, the first time I sat in front of a computer as a kid.
That curiosity carried me from Java engineering in Morocco to the Salesforce platform, and
eventually to **Dubai** — with a strong coffee (or a mint tea) never far away.

One-liner: *software engineer — building & teaching on the Salesforce Platform @ Salesforce.*

Statement: *I turn ideas into software people actually love: clean code, clear teaching, and
the small details that quietly do the work.*
